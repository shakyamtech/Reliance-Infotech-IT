03 Accordion / FAQ

How do I get an IT infrastructure quote?
Simply submit your requirements through our contact page or call us directly. Our engineers will arrange a free on-site survey.
What products do you supply?
We supply business laptops, desktops, servers, printers, Cat6 network cables, Cisco/Mikrotik routers, and CCTV systems.
Are all hardware parts covered by warranty?
Yes, all products carry official OEM manufacturer warranties with local service center backing.
Do you offer emergency on-site AMC support?
Yes. AMC contract clients receive rapid on-site technician dispatch within 2-4 hours inside Kathmandu Valley.
Can you set up multi-branch VPN connections?
Yes, we regularly configure secure IPsec and WireGuard site-to-site VPN tunnels for multi-office organizations.
Do you accept Annual Maintenance Contracts for existing setups?
Yes. We conduct an initial health audit of your existing hardware and networks before onboarding onto our AMC tier.
