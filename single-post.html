Guide to Structured Cabling & Wi-Fi 6 Upgrades

August 2026 Network Engineering Team 5 min read

In today's cloud-dependent workspace, a slow or unreliable local network bottlenecks your entire team's productivity. Here is our engineering team's comprehensive checklist for upgrading office network infrastructure.

1. Upgrade from Cat5e to Pure Copper Cat6 / Cat6A

Many legacy office setups still run on copper-clad aluminum (CCA) Cat5e cables that suffer from significant packet drops and high resistance over distance. Pure copper Cat6 or Cat6A delivers full Gigabit and 10-Gigabit throughput with superior crosstalk resistance.

2. Implement Centralized Patch Panels & Server Racks

Loose cables running across floors or tangled behind desks increase the risk of accidental disconnects and hardware damage. A dedicated 19-inch wall-mount or floor-standing server rack with numbered patch panels makes troubleshooting simple and organized.

"A well-structured network isn't just about speed — it's about eliminating downtime and ensuring seamless scalability as your company grows."

— Reliance Infotech Engineering Team

3. Deploy High-Density Wi-Fi 6 Access Points (OFDMA & MU-MIMO)

Consumer-grade wireless routers quickly choke when 30+ laptops, phones, and IoT devices connect simultaneously. Enterprise Wi-Fi 6 access points with centralized cloud/hardware controllers distribute load evenly, maintaining low ping and high bandwidth throughout the office.

4. Segment Your Traffic With VLANs

Separate staff workstations, VoIP phone systems, IP CCTV security cameras, and guest Wi-Fi onto dedicated Virtual LANs (VLANs). This boosts network security and prevents video streams or guest traffic from congesting critical business operations.

5. Install Redundant Power & Multi-WAN Failover

Pair your core router with multi-WAN failover (dual ISP connectivity) and connect all switches and NVR units to a reliable online UPS. Even during power cuts or ISP outages, your core team stays online without interruption.
