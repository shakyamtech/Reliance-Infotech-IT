What does an Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) cover?
Our AMC covers scheduled preventive maintenance, hardware diagnostics, network speed checks, software OS patching, virus cleanup, and rapid emergency on-site technician response.
Do you supply genuine computer hardware and parts with warranty?
Yes. All laptops, desktops, servers, switches, and CCTV cameras are 100% genuine and sourced directly with official manufacturer warranties and authorized service support.
How fast can a technician arrive for emergency network outages?
For AMC clients inside the Kathmandu Valley, our emergency response team provides remote diagnostics within 15 minutes and physical on-site presence within 2 to 4 hours.
Can you design and cable an entire new office from scratch?
Absolutely. We specialize in turn-key office setups: structured Cat6 cabling, server racks, managed Wi-Fi access points, router firewalls, CCTV, and biometric doors.
